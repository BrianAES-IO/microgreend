What Are Microgreens?

Small Plants,
Extraordinary Power

Microgreens are the young, tender seedlings of vegetables and herbs, harvested just days after germination. Research shows they contain up to 40 times the nutrients of their mature counterparts — making them one of the most potent superfoods on the planet.

At Micro-Greens Farm Ja, we bring this nutritional powerhouse to Jamaican tables for the first time — grown locally, harvested fresh, and delivered straight to you.
