Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

When do you harvest?

We harvest every Saturday morning and deliver the same day. This guarantees what you receive is at peak freshness — cut and at your door within hours, never stored overnight.

When is the order deadline?

Orders must be placed by Friday at 12:00 noon to be included in that Saturday's harvest. Orders received after Friday noon will be prepared for the following Saturday.

Do you offer pickup?

No — we are a delivery-only service. We deliver directly to your door across Jamaica. There is a flat J$500 delivery fee per order.

Which parishes do you deliver to?

We deliver across all 14 parishes of Jamaica. Simply enter your delivery address when placing your order and we'll confirm delivery to your area.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer and cash on delivery. After placing your order online, we will contact you to confirm and provide bank transfer details if applicable. Cash on delivery is available at no extra charge.

How long do microgreens stay fresh?

Properly refrigerated, our microgreens stay fresh for 5–7 days. Store them in their packaging with a slightly damp paper towel and refrigerate immediately after delivery. Do not wash until just before serving.

Do you supply restaurants and businesses?

Yes! We welcome wholesale and restaurant enquiries. Use the contact form above and select "Wholesale / Restaurant" as your subject, or reach out directly via WhatsApp or Instagram. We'll discuss your volume requirements and pricing.

Are your microgreens organic?

We do not use pesticides, herbicides, or synthetic fertilisers. We grow in premium, clean growing media and use only filtered water. While we are not formally certified organic, our growing practices are fully clean and natural.
