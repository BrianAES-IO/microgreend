1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit.

Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work more efficiently, to provide reporting information, and to support a better user experience. Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Micro-Greens Farm Ja) are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called third-party cookies.

Similar technologies — such as web storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) and pixel tags — operate in a comparable way and are also covered by this Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operations — to keep your shopping cart active, maintain session state, and protect against cross-site request forgery.
  • Preference memory — to remember choices you have made so you do not have to re-enter them each visit (for example, dismissed notice banners).
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. Data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
  • Marketing — to show you relevant advertisements on social media and other platforms, and to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns.

We will only place non-essential cookies with your explicit consent, in accordance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act 2020, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

3. Cookie Categories

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They enable core features such as your shopping cart, security controls, and session continuity. The website cannot function properly without them and they cannot be disabled through our consent tool.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest / technical necessity (no consent required).

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as dismissing a notice banner) and provide enhanced, personalised features. They do not track your activity across other websites.

Legal basis: Your consent.

3.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve the site's structure, content, and performance.

Legal basis: Your consent.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your browsing activity across websites to allow advertising partners to deliver relevant advertisements, and to limit the number of times you see an ad. They may share this information with other organisations such as social media platforms.

Legal basis: Your consent.

6. Local Storage & Similar Technologies

In addition to traditional HTTP cookies, our site uses browser Web Storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). Unlike cookies, Web Storage data is not automatically transmitted to our servers — it remains entirely on your device.

  • localStorage — persists until you clear your browser's storage. Survives browser restarts.
  • sessionStorage — lasts only for the duration of your browser session. Cleared automatically when you close the tab or window.

All data held in localStorage under our keys (mgfj_*) stays on your device and is never transmitted to any external server by us. You can clear it at any time through your browser's storage settings or developer tools.

8. Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View cookies and Web Storage stored by websites.
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is placed.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites. The shopping cart, for example, requires localStorage to work.

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

For opt-out tools from advertising networks, visit Your Online Choices (EU) or Digital Advertising Alliance (US).
