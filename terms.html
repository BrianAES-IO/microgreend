6. Product Quality & Freshness

6.1 Freshness Guarantee

Our microgreens are harvested on Saturday morning and delivered the same day, ensuring maximum freshness. Properly stored (refrigerated, in their original packaging), our microgreens will remain fresh for 5–7 days.

6.2 Storage Instructions

  • Refrigerate immediately upon receipt.
  • Do not wash until just before use.
  • Keep in their original sealed packaging with a slightly damp paper towel.
  • Store away from strong-smelling foods.

6.3 Organic Status

Our microgreens are grown without pesticides, herbicides, or synthetic fertilisers in clean growing media with filtered water. We are not formally certified organic by any certification body. References to "clean" or "natural" growing practices on our website describe our methods and do not constitute an organic certification claim.
