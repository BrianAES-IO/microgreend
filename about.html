How It Began

Born from a
Simple Question

Why are the most nutrient-dense foods in the world unavailable in Jamaica?

Microgreens have been used by Michelin-starred chefs and health-conscious families around the world for years. Yet in Jamaica — a country rich with sunshine, fertile soil, and a culture that has always celebrated good food — they were nowhere to be found.

That question sparked a journey. We researched, experimented, failed, learned, and refined — until we grew our first successful tray of microgreens right here on Jamaican soil. That was the beginning of Micro-Greens Farm Ja.

Today, we grow more than a dozen varieties in our carefully controlled growing environment, harvesting every Saturday morning and delivering to your door the same day — so every order arrives at its absolute freshest.

How We Grow

From Seed to
Your Hands

Our growing process is designed around one principle: maximum nutrition, minimum intervention. Here is how each tray makes its journey from seed to your table.

01

Seed Selection & Preparation

We source only premium, non-GMO seeds. Each batch is soaked in filtered water for 6–12 hours to begin the germination process and activate the seed's natural enzymes.

02

Planting by Hand

Seeds are evenly distributed by hand onto trays of premium growing media — chosen for its clean, neutral composition that lets the plant's natural flavours shine through.

03

Controlled Environment Growth

Our growing environment maintains optimal temperature, humidity, and airflow. The first 2–3 days, trays are kept in darkness (blackout) to encourage strong root development — mimicking natural soil cover.

04

Light & Daily Care

Once sprouted, trays are moved under our growing lights and watered twice daily from below — never on the leaves — to prevent mould and maximise nutrient uptake.

05

Saturday Harvest

Each variety is harvested at its precise peak — typically 7–14 days after seeding — when the first true leaves emerge and nutrient density is at its highest. We harvest every Saturday morning.

06

Fresh Packaging & Delivery

Harvested greens are packed immediately and delivered to your door the same day. No storage, no cold chain delays — just farm-fresh microgreens at their absolute best.
